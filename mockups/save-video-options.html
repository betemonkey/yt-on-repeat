On repeat · save audio or video

One save button, audio or video

The download button beside "Open on YouTube" stays the only save button. It now offers Audio (MP3, as today) or Video (MP4). Video goes to its own folder, Videos\On repeat. It uses the same yt-dlp and ffmpeg the app already fetches, so nothing new is downloaded. Windows app only, like Save audio.

Why these choices

MP4 always, so it plays everywhere, including on the phone and in Jellyfin. 1080p by default: "Best" can be 4K, which is big and sometimes isn't available as MP4, so it may come out as WebM/MKV instead.

Video is saved with title, channel and cover image, like audio. Your audio settings stay as they are today.

Save all opens the same choice. Each kind goes into a subfolder named after the list (Music\On repeat\Focus, Videos\On repeat\Focus), and songs already saved that way are skipped.

To decide

  • V1, V2 or V3? My pick is V1: it's closest to "a button that offers MP3 or video", and you never save the wrong kind by accident.
  • Default video quality? My pick is 1080p MP4: it looks good and the files stay reasonable.
  • Video folder? My pick is Videos\On repeat, kept apart from the music.
