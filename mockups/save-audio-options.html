Saving a song's audio

One new button beside "Open on YouTube" saves the song you're playing as an audio file, with its title, channel and cover art. The same "Save audio" is in each row's + menu, and a playlist gets "Save all". The Windows app does the work with yt-dlp and ffmpeg; the phone version doesn't get it.

To decide

  • S1 or S2? My pick is S1: it matches the buttons beside it and leaves the title its room.
  • Default format? My pick is MP3 because it plays everywhere. M4A and Opus keep YouTube's audio without converting it, so the quality is a bit better and the files are smaller.
  • Tools fetched on first use, or included in the download? My pick is first use: the release stays small and yt-dlp can keep itself up to date.
